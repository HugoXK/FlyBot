A message you send while a Bot is stuck in a long foreground command or job wait now reaches it instead of sitting in the queue: once that wait has lasted more than a minute, the turn is stopped the way the Stop button stops it, the wait is cut short, and your message opens the next turn at once. Short commands still run to completion, and a turn that is already asking you a question is left alone. ([#1509](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1509))
