The Bot exchange sheet showed and copied a stored turn's routing along with its words: a leading `Chief → 助手` delegation trace, and a remote writer's `<untrusted-message …>` fence, appeared in the bubble and in Copy as if the writer had typed them. Every stored line now goes through one reader, `exchangeText()`, which unwraps the fence while keeping its line breaks, drops the leading trace and the leftover fence fragments, and trims only the ends — so the paragraph breaks of a multi-paragraph reply survive, and the displayed text and the copied text are the same ([#1423](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1423)).
