A conversation part that a job still held when the Bot moved on to a fresh part is archived once that job ends, instead of staying active for the life of the instance. Nothing is force-stopped: a job, turn or subagent is left to finish, and the part is asked for once more, with no options, when its holder is reported ended — the job registry's own settlement, or the Session's next idle — keeping the id while a job, turn or subagent still holds it. A scheduled task keeps the existing stop-then-archive path ([#896](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/896), [#1433](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/1433)).
